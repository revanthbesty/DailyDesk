FAQ

Before you make the switch.

Simple answers to the questions a new user is likely to have.

What does DailyDesk Free include?

Movement, eye and hydration reminders, custom intervals, break durations, Quick Setup presets, pause controls, Daily Boost and personal reminders/routines.

What exactly does Pro add?

Pro adds the four current wellness tools, multiple custom schedules, the 17-theme visual palette and account-based Pro restore, plus future premium improvements as they are released.

Why should I choose Quarterly or Yearly?

Longer plans reduce the effective monthly cost. Quarterly saves ₹48 versus three monthly payments; Yearly saves ₹289 versus twelve monthly payments.

What makes Lifetime different?

Lifetime is a one-time ₹599 purchase with no recurring payment. Compared with paying ₹49 monthly, it saves ₹577 over 24 months and reaches its payback point in roughly 13 months.

Can I use Pro on another computer?

Yes—the account-based design is intended to let you restore an active Pro entitlement on another device using the purchase email.

Do I need an account to use DailyDesk Free?

No. The Free experience remains designed to work without an account.

Are Cloud Sync and AI available today?

Not yet. They are planned features and are clearly labelled as planned on this website.

Which browsers will DailyDesk support?

DailyDesk is being built around the Chromium extension model, starting with Chrome and targeting compatible Chromium browsers next. Each browser store may have its own publishing and compatibility requirements.
