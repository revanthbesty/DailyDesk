FAQ

Good questions before you install.

Does DailyDesk need an account?

No. DailyDesk Free is designed to work without an account. Pro uses an account-style purchase identity so you can restore your entitlement on another device.

How does Pro restore work?

For the current development flow, enter the purchase email in DailyDesk and the service checks the active Pro entitlement. Email verification can be added as the production account system is finalized.

What happens when I change devices?

Your Pro purchase is tied to the purchase identity rather than a specific computer. Install DailyDesk on the new device and use Restore Pro.

Will DailyDesk interrupt meetings?

You can pause reminders, and the extension is designed around lightweight notifications and quick controls rather than constant on-screen interruption.

What is planned for the future?

Cloud sync and AI-powered insights are planned additions to the Pro experience. They are not presented as currently available features.

Is DailyDesk available on Chrome?

Yes. DailyDesk is built as a Chrome extension. The production Chrome Web Store link will be connected once the store listing is published.
