FAQ

Everything you need to know before upgrading.

Does DailyDesk Free require an account?

No. The Free experience is designed to work without an account.

What does Pro actually add?

Pro adds wellness prompts, multiple custom schedule control, the full theme palette, account-based Pro restore and other premium features as they are released.

What wellness tools are included?

Pro currently includes Blink & Eye Reset, Posture Check, Breathing Reset and Short Walk prompts. They appear as short animated wellness popups.

Can I change devices after buying Pro?

The account-based restore design allows a Pro entitlement to be restored on another device using the purchase email rather than asking the customer for a Razorpay payment ID.

Can I pause DailyDesk?

Yes. Quick controls let you pause reminders for 30 minutes, 1 hour or until tomorrow.

How do personal reminders work?

You can create one-time or repeating reminders, choose how early DailyDesk should notify you and manage routines such as meetings and tasks.

Is cloud sync available now?

Not yet. Cloud sync is a planned feature and is intentionally labelled as planned on this website.

Is AI included now?

Not yet. AI-powered insights are planned for a future Pro release and are not presented as currently available.
