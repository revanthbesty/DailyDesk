Overview

DailyDesk is designed as a local-first Chrome extension. Reminder settings and day-to-day local reminder data are intended to remain on the user's device.

Information collected

The Free experience does not require an account. The Pro activation system may process a purchase email and entitlement information so a valid Pro purchase can be restored on another device.

Payments

Payments are processed by the selected payment provider. DailyDesk uses entitlement information supplied to its backend to determine whether a Pro purchase is active.

Future features

Cloud sync and AI-powered insights are planned separately. This policy will be updated before additional personal data is collected for those capabilities.

Purchase & refund policy

All DailyDesk Pro purchases are final and non-refundable, except where a refund is required by applicable law or by the payment provider's rules. Please review the selected plan, price and included features before purchase.
