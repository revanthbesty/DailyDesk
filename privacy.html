PRIVACY & PURCHASE POLICY

Clear policies. No surprises.

DailyDesk is designed to keep the everyday experience simple and local-first. This page explains what the product is designed to store, what Pro activation requires, and the current purchase and refund policy.

1 · OVERVIEW

Built to be useful without being invasive.

DailyDesk is intended to function as a local-first browser extension. Core reminder settings and day-to-day reminder behavior are designed to remain on the user's device rather than requiring a cloud account.

2 · INFORMATION

What the product may process.

The Free experience does not require an account. For Pro purchases, the activation system may process the purchase email and entitlement information needed to confirm an active purchase and restore Pro on another device.

  • Local reminder and preference data is designed to stay on the device.
  • Pro entitlement records may include purchase email, plan, payment reference and entitlement dates.
  • The product does not need the contents of your personal reminders in order to verify a Pro purchase.
3 · PAYMENTS

Payments are processed by the payment provider.

DailyDesk uses its payment provider to process purchases. The DailyDesk backend uses entitlement information supplied by the payment flow to determine whether Pro access is active. Payment credentials are handled by the payment provider rather than stored as part of the DailyDesk extension.

4 · PRO RESTORE

Your Pro purchase is tied to the purchase identity.

The account-based Pro design is intended to let an eligible customer restore an active Pro entitlement on another compatible device using the purchase email associated with the entitlement. The customer should not need to manually enter a Razorpay payment ID for normal restore.

5 · PURCHASE POLICY

No-refund policy for DailyDesk Pro.

All DailyDesk Pro purchases are final and non-refundable, except where a refund is required by applicable law or by the payment provider's rules. Please review the selected plan, price and included Pro features before completing a purchase.

6 · FUTURE FEATURES

Future data use will be disclosed before expansion.

Cloud Sync and AI-powered insights are planned features. This policy will be updated before those capabilities are introduced in a way that requires additional personal or usage data.

7 · CONTACT

Need help with a purchase or policy question?

Before production publishing, replace the placeholder support address in the website footer with the official DailyDesk support email.
